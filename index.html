M5StickS3 · Wi‑Fi defenseLive · v1.0.0

M5 Guardian

A pocket radio guard for your own Wi‑Fi.

It listens to the 2.4 GHz air around one network you pick and shows on its screen how that network advertises itself: five faces rate the security settings, a red ALERT marks an incident on your network, amber marks something nearby. Receive-only, signed updates, installed from the browser.

  • ESP-IDF
  • Web installer
  • MIT

StickS3 + ENV III · Grow roomsLive

Doutor VPD

Air VPD, measured where the plants are.

A StickS3 with an ENV III sensor computes air VPD on the device, compares it with the target for each growth stage and keeps minute history, time in target and graphs. No phone, account or network needed.

  • Signed firmware
  • Site in Portuguese

ESP32-S3 · AndroidLive

Droidputer

Cardputer apps, with your phone as the screen.

Any open-source Cardputer app, rebuilt unchanged against a small shim, runs on an ESP32-S3 while an Android phone over USB-OTG becomes its screen, keyboard, GPS and flasher. No display needed on the board.

  • USB-OTG
  • MIT

Open data · ESP32 familyLive

esp-atlas

Which ESP32 for what you're building?

A community-maintained, datasheet-verified catalog of ESP32 chips, modules, dev boards and the firmware that runs on them. Every hard spec cites an official source, and the data is plain markdown anyone can correct by pull request.

Stackfall

Tilt to steer, dip to rotate.

An original falling-block puzzle for one StickS3: tilt the stick to steer, dip it to rotate and soft-drop, SIDE hard-drops. The engine is pure C++17 and host-tested, and an autopilot can play it over USB.
